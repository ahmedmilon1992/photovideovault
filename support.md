# Support — Photo & Video Vault

## Quick answers

**I forgot my PIN / password / pattern.**
On the lock screen choose *Forgot?* and enter your recovery key (the 52-character code shown when you set up the vault). You will set a new secret. Without the secret or the recovery key, the vault cannot be opened by anyone — not even us. That is the point of the app.

**Where is the second (hidden) vault?**
Tap the purple shield icon in the main vault, or type the hidden vault's own secret on the lock screen. The hidden vault is part of Pro.

**Is my data uploaded anywhere?**
No. Everything stays encrypted on your iPhone. iCloud backup is optional and copies only encrypted files into your own iCloud Drive.

**How do I move my vault to a new iPhone?**
See the Restore guide: https://ahmedmilon1992.github.io/photovideovault/restore

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Photo & Video Vault → Cancel.

**Something isn't working.**
Open an issue: https://github.com/ahmedmilon1992/photovideovault/issues — include your iOS version and what you tapped. Never include your secret or recovery key; support will never ask for them.
