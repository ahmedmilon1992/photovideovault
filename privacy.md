# Privacy Policy — Photo & Video Vault

**Effective date:** October 10, 2026

Photo & Video Vault ("the app") is made by Primecell Distributor LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **your photos, videos and files never leave your device unless you choose to export or back them up, and we do not collect any data about you.**

## What the app stores
- Photos, videos, Live Photos, GIFs, PDFs, notes and voice memos you add to the vault are encrypted on your iPhone with keys derived from your secret (PIN, password or pattern). They are stored only on your device.
- Your secret and recovery key are never stored in readable form and are never sent anywhere. If you lose both, nobody — including us — can recover your vault.
- Optional on-device text recognition (private search) runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. Results are stored inside your encrypted vault.
- The break-in report records the time and method of failed unlock attempts on your device. It does not take photos or record location.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no servers of ours. We cannot see your files, your secret, your usage, or your identity.

## Optional iCloud backup
If you turn on iCloud backup, the app copies your **encrypted** vault files into your own iCloud Drive, under your Apple ID. Apple stores that data according to Apple's privacy policy. The files remain encrypted with your keys; Apple and we cannot read them.

## Purchases
Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the transaction.

## Permissions the app asks for
- **Photos** — only to remove originals you choose to delete after a verified import. Selecting photos uses Apple's picker and does not require this permission.
- **Camera** and **Microphone** — only when you use the private camera, scanner or voice memos.
- **Face ID** — only if you enable biometric unlock.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

We will post updates to this page and change the effective date above.
